Video messaging has become a core business tool. Instead of writing long emails or scheduling meetings, teams record quick videos to explain concepts, walk through proposals, or provide feedback. Loom and Vidyard are the two dominant platforms in this space, and while they look similar on the surface, they serve very different use cases. Loom focuses on internal team communication and async collaboration. Vidyard focuses on sales prospecting and video marketing. This comparison explains the differences so you can pick the right one.

## Company Focus and Philosophy

**Loom** was built for teams. The original use case was simple: record your screen, share a link, and let colleagues watch whenever they want. Loom has evolved into a comprehensive async communication platform with features for team collaboration, commenting, and knowledge sharing. In 2024, Loom was acquired by Atlassian, which has integrated it more deeply with Jira, Confluence, and the Atlassian ecosystem.

**Vidyard** was built for sales and marketing. The original use case was tracking who watched your sales videos and using that data to prioritize follow-ups. Vidyard has evolved into a video platform for sales prospecting, marketing campaigns, and customer communication. Vidyard's strength is in the analytics and CRM integration that help sales teams close deals.

## Recording Experience

**Loom** provides one of the simplest recording experiences available. You click the Loom extension or desktop app, choose to record your screen, your camera, or both, and start recording. The recording interface is minimal and does not get in the way. Loom supports recording up to 4K resolution, and the processing is fast. You get a shareable link within seconds of stopping the recording.

**Vidyard** offers a similar recording experience with additional options. Vidyard supports screen recording, camera recording, and combination recordings. Vidyard's recorder includes teleprompter mode, which displays a script while you record — useful for sales videos where you want to appear polished. Vidyard also supports multi-scene videos, letting you combine multiple clips into a single video without external editing software.

**Recording verdict:** Loom is faster and simpler for quick team videos. Vidyard is better for polished sales videos that benefit from teleprompter and multi-scene features.

## AI Features

Both platforms have invested heavily in AI, but for different purposes.

**Loom** uses AI to make async communication more efficient. AI generates video titles, summaries, and action items from your recordings. The AI meeting notes feature creates structured summaries of recorded meetings. Loom's AI can generate chapters for long videos, create transcripts, and suggest follow-up messages. The AI features are focused on making video content more searchable and actionable for teams.

**Vidyard** uses AI to improve sales performance. AI generates video scripts, suggests personalization based on prospect data, and creates follow-up messages. Vidyard's AI can generate multiple versions of a video script for A/B testing, create personalized video thumbnails, and analyze engagement patterns to suggest optimal send times. The AI features are focused on improving conversion rates and sales productivity.

**AI verdict:** Loom's AI is better for team communication. Vidyard's AI is better for sales outreach.

## Analytics and Tracking

This is where Vidyard pulls significantly ahead.

**Loom** provides view analytics including who watched your video, how much they watched, and when they watched it. You can see engagement heatmaps that show which parts of the video were rewatched or skipped. For team use, these analytics are sufficient for understanding whether colleagues engaged with your video.

**Vidyard** provides detailed sales-oriented analytics. You can see who watched your video, how much they watched, which specific moments they engaged with, and whether they shared the video with others. Vidyard integrates with CRM systems to automatically log video engagement data, so sales teams can see which prospects are most engaged. Vidyard's analytics also include viewer identification, which can identify anonymous viewers by their email address when they interact with your video.

**Analytics verdict:** Vidyard wins decisively for sales use cases. Loom's analytics are adequate for internal team use.

## Integrations

**Loom** has deep integration with the Atlassian ecosystem (Jira, Confluence, Trello), Slack, Microsoft Teams, Notion, and Google Workspace. Loom videos can be embedded directly in Jira tickets, Confluence pages, and Notion documents. The Atlassian acquisition has made Loom the video layer for Atlassian's productivity suite.

**Vidyard** has deep integration with CRM and sales tools. Vidyard connects natively with Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, Outreach, Salesloft, and other sales platforms. Video engagement data flows directly into your CRM, enriching lead scores and triggering automated follow-up sequences. Vidyard also integrates with email platforms for video-in-email campaigns.

**Integrations verdict:** Choose based on your workflow. Loom integrates better with project management and team collaboration tools. Vidyard integrates better with sales and marketing tools.

## Video Hosting and Sharing

**Loom** hosts your videos on its platform and provides shareable links. Videos can be embedded on websites, shared in Slack/Teams messages, or accessed through the Loom library. Loom supports password protection, domain restriction, and expiration dates for shared links. The free tier limits video length to 5 minutes (previously unlimited, but Loom changed this in 2024).

**Vidyard** hosts videos on its platform with additional sharing options. Videos can be shared via links, embedded on websites, or sent through email with tracking. Vidyard supports video playlists, channels, and custom branding (your logo, colors, and domain). The free tier limits video length to 30 minutes.

**Hosting verdict:** Vidyard offers more professional hosting features (branding, playlists, custom domain). Loom offers simpler sharing for internal use.

## Pricing in 2026

**Loom** has a free tier that includes 25 videos per person, 5-minute maximum length, and basic analytics. The Business plan costs $15 per user per month and includes unlimited videos, unlimited length, custom branding, and advanced analytics. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing with SSO, SCIM, and admin controls.

**Vidyard** has a free tier that includes 25 videos, 30-minute maximum length, and basic analytics. The Pro plan costs $59 per month (individual) and includes unlimited videos, custom branding, and detailed analytics. The Plus plan is $99 per user per month with CRM integration, team features, and advanced analytics. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing.

**Price verdict:** Loom is significantly cheaper for team use. Vidyard's pricing is justified for sales teams that benefit from CRM integration and advanced analytics, but it is expensive for non-sales use cases.

## Use Cases: Sales and Prospecting

**Vidyard** is the clear winner for sales teams. The combination of personalized video prospecting, CRM integration, viewer tracking, and automated follow-ups makes Vidyard a complete sales video platform. Sales teams can record personalized demo videos, track which prospects engage with them, and automate follow-up sequences based on viewer behavior. Vidyard's video email feature lets you embed videos directly in email campaigns with tracking.

**Loom** can be used for sales, but it lacks the sales-specific features that make Vidyard effective. Loom does not have CRM integration, automated follow-up sequences, or viewer identification for anonymous prospects. For informal sales communication (explaining a proposal, walking through a contract), Loom works well. For structured sales processes, Vidyard is the better tool.

## Use Cases: Internal Team Communication

**Loom** is the clear winner for internal communication. The Atlassian integration means Loom videos live alongside Jira tickets and Confluence docs. Loom's commenting system lets team members leave timestamped feedback on videos. The async-first approach reduces meeting overhead. Loom's AI summaries make long recordings searchable and actionable.

**Vidyard** can be used for internal communication, but it lacks the team collaboration features that make Loom effective. Vidyard does not have commenting, @mentions, or deep integration with project management tools. For sharing knowledge across teams, Loom is the better tool.

## Use Cases: Customer Support

Both platforms work for customer support, but differently.

**Loom** is better for support teams that create knowledge base content. Loom videos can be embedded in Confluence pages, Notion docs, or help center articles. The AI-generated summaries make videos searchable. Loom's simplicity means support agents can record and share solutions quickly.

**Vidyard** is better for support teams that need to track customer engagement. Vidyard's analytics show which support videos customers actually watch, helping teams improve their content. Vidyard's integration with CRM systems means support interactions are logged alongside sales data.

## When to Choose Loom

Loom is the better choice when:

- **Your primary use case is internal team communication.** Loom's commenting, Atlassian integration, and async features make it ideal for team collaboration.
- **You need a simple, fast recording experience.** Loom's minimal interface gets out of the way.
- **Budget matters** and you need video messaging for a large team.
- **You use the Atlassian ecosystem** (Jira, Confluence, Trello) and want seamless video integration.

## When to Choose Vidyard

Vidyard is the better choice when:

- **Your primary use case is sales and prospecting.** Vidyard's CRM integration, viewer tracking, and automated follow-ups are purpose-built for sales.
- **You need detailed engagement analytics** to prioritize follow-ups and measure content effectiveness.
- **You want professional video hosting** with custom branding, playlists, and domain customization.
- **You send video emails at scale** and need tracking and automation features.

## The Verdict

Loom and Vidyard serve different markets despite looking similar on the surface. Loom is the better tool for internal team communication, async collaboration, and knowledge sharing. Vidyard is the better tool for sales prospecting, marketing campaigns, and customer-facing video communication. The pricing difference is significant — Loom is much cheaper for team use. Choose based on your primary use case: if you are communicating internally, choose Loom. If you are selling externally, choose Vidyard.
